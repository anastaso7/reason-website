What we do

Three ways in. Choose how you want to train.

24/7 Open Gym

Your own space to train at any hour. Never more than three people at once, quality equipment and no waiting. Members book a slot in the Fitbook app and unlock the door from their phone.

Drop-in €20 · Memberships from €50 a month

Strength & Conditioning groups

Coached sessions in groups of up to six: lift, carry, push, pull. Scaled to you, one focused hour at a time.

First session free · drop-in €20 · memberships from €55

Personal sessions

One-to-one or in pairs. Attentive, technique-first coaching and a plan shaped around your body and your goals.

€40 solo · €50 for two

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Can't find what you need? Message us on Instagram and we'll help.

Of course. Try the open gym with a single drop-in session, or join a group training session free before you choose a membership.

Groups are limited to six people, and the open gym allows a maximum of three at any time, a limit that is rarely even reached. That means a relaxed space, no crowds and no waiting for equipment.

The gym is open 24/7 with a booking, but it is not staffed around the clock. For a membership, or any question before you join, message us on Instagram.

With an active booking, a lock icon appears in the top-right corner of the Fitbook app. Tap it and the entrance unlocks automatically. Access opens 15 minutes before your session starts and closes 15 minutes after it ends.

Members use the Fitbook app: choose your date and time and reserve your spot in a few taps. If you are new, message us on WhatsApp and we will set you up.

You can pay at the gym, or online with a payment link we send you. The link is also in our Instagram bio.

Group sessions last about an hour. Open gym sessions are 60 minutes, with a little extra time before and after to get ready.
